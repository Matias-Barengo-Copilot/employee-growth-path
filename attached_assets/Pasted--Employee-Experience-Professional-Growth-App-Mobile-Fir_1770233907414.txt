# Employee Experience & Professional Growth App (Mobile-First) — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1) Overview

A mobile-first, polished “work companion” app for small agency teams (10–20 people) to strengthen culture, increase role clarity/accountability, and support continuous professional growth.

The app combines:

* **Connection & culture** (Directory, Snaps, Social Board)
* **Growth & accountability** (OKRs/Goals, Professional Challenges)
* **Feedback & reflection** (Peer/Supervisor feedback, Quarterly self-reflection)
* **Lightweight org health signal** (Pulse Check mood tracking)
* **Mobile-native input** (Voice notes → transcription → structured data)
* **Operational visibility** (OOO/Vacation)

Teams: Copilot Innovations (AI innovation) + Phase 3 Digital (digital agency)

---

## 2) Goals

### Primary goals

1. **Build stronger culture and connection** across small teams.
2. **Increase accountability and clarity**: roles, expectations, goals, and success criteria are visible and trackable.
3. **Create a healthy feedback loop**: easy to give/request feedback from peers and managers.
4. **Encourage professional growth** through nudges, challenges, and reflections.
5. **Make participation effortless** via mobile-first UX and voice capture.

### Secondary goals

* Reduce reliance on scattered tools for “people ops” signals.
* Create a lightweight, positive ritual that complements Slack rather than competing with it.

---

## 3) Non-Goals (for initial versions)

* Full HRIS replacement (payroll, benefits, compliance).
* Formal performance review workflows with compensation decisions.
* Heavy project management or task tracking (Jira/Asana replacement).
* Company-wide knowledge base/wiki.

---

## 4) Target Users & Personas

1. **Individual Contributor (IC)**

   * Wants quick recognition, clarity on goals, easy ways to ask for feedback, and growth guidance.
2. **Team Lead / Manager**

   * Wants visibility into team goals progress, lightweight sentiment signals, and a simple feedback cadence.
3. **Agency Owner / Operator (Admin)**

   * Wants culture flywheel, organizational health trends, and adoption analytics.

---

## 5) Experience Principles

* **Apple-like polish**: clean, minimal, calm; no “prototype” feel.
* **Low friction**: interactions should take seconds.
* **Psychological safety**: privacy-first defaults, clear anonymity rules.
* **Positive energy**: recognition-forward, growth-forward.
* **Mobile-native**: voice, camera, and push notifications are first-class.

---

## 6) MVP Focus (Phase 1)

The MVP must deliver immediate value and habit formation with a focused scope.

### MVP Features (in scope)

1. **Employee Directory (Enhanced)**
2. **OKRs & Goal Tracker**
3. **Giving “Snaps” (Recognition)**
4. **Peer Feedback**

   * Provide feedback
   * Request feedback
   * Optional anonymity

### MVP Success criteria (initial)

* ≥70% of team members complete their profile
* ≥60% of team members give at least one Snap per month
* ≥50% of team members create at least one goal within first 30 days
* ≥30% of team members either request or provide peer feedback within first 45 days

### MVP explicitly out of scope (Phase 2+)

* Social Board
* Pulse Check mood tracker
* Professional Challenges
* Voice notes → transcription → structured mapping
* Supervisor/manager feedback workflows
* Quarterly self-reflection / self-eval
* OOO/Vacation calendar

---

## 8) Functional Requirements (Detailed)

### 8.1 Employee Directory (Enhanced) — MVP

**Purpose:** Make it easy (and enjoyable) to find people, understand roles, and build connection.

**Key fields (baseline):**

* Name, photo, title/role, team, manager
* Location/timezone (optional)
* Contact links (Slack, email)

**Enhanced fields (culture + context):**

* “What I do” (short)
* Strengths / superpowers (tag list)
* Fun facts (prompt-based)
* Working preferences (e.g., focus hours, communication style)
* “Currently working on” (short)

**Core interactions:**

* Search (name/role/skills)
* Filter by team/discipline
* View profile card and full profile

**Admin controls:**

* Add/remove users
* Control which fields are required vs optional

---

### 8.2 OKRs & Goal Tracker — MVP

**Purpose:** Provide clarity and accountability around what success looks like.

**Objects:**

* Goal (personal)
* OKR (optional structure; can be enabled per org)

**Goal fields:**

* Title, description
* Category (Growth / Delivery / Leadership / Learning)
* Timeframe (Quarterly default)
* Status (Not started / On track / At risk / Completed)
* Progress indicator (manual % or checklist)
* Visibility (Private / Manager / Team)

**Core flows:**

* Create/edit goal
* Weekly quick update (lightweight)
* Goal check-in reminders (configurable)
* View: personal dashboard + optional team view (based on visibility)

**Optional (later):** link goals to feedback entries or reflection.

---

### 8.3 “Snaps” (Recognition) — MVP

**Purpose:** Create frequent, lightweight recognition moments.

**Snap composition:**

* Recipient(s)
* Message (short)
* Optional tags (e.g., Collaboration, Craft, Initiative)
* Optional attachment (photo/GIF) (optional for MVP)

**Core flows:**

* Give a Snap in <15 seconds
* Recipient notified (push/in-app)
* Snap appears in recipient profile (and optionally a recognition feed)

**Constraints:**

* Default: positive recognition only (no “constructive snaps”)
* Rate limiting optional (prevent spam)

---

### 8.4 Peer Feedback — MVP

**Purpose:** Normalize continuous feedback with safety and structure.

**Feedback types:**

* Peer-to-peer

**Two primary actions:**

1. **Provide feedback**
2. **Request feedback**

**Feedback structure (recommended):**

* “Keep doing”
* “Consider improving”
* Optional tags (Communication, Quality, Ownership, Teamwork)

**Anonymity options:**

* Toggle: **Anonymous to recipient** (but visible to Admin for safety/audit)
* Or: Anonymous to everyone except system (policy-driven)

**Request feedback flow:**

* Select person(s)
* Choose prompt template (e.g., “How can I improve in client communication?”)
* Optional deadline
* Nudges/reminders

**Recipient experience:**

* Clean inbox of feedback
* Ability to mark as read, save, or add private notes
* Option to request clarification (non-anonymous cases)

**Safety/abuse handling:**

* Report feedback, block user (admin review)
* Clear norms and tone guidelines

---

## 9) Phase 2+ Requirements (Non-MVP)

### 9.1 Social / Community Board (Fun, Friendly)

**Purpose:** A lightweight, visual culture feed that complements Slack.

* Photo-first posts (e.g., vibe-working)
* “Cool thing I shipped” / “Today’s win” / “Behind the scenes”
* Reactions (emoji), comments (optional), share to Slack (optional)

### 9.2 Pulse Check (Mood Tracker)

**Purpose:** 1-question check-in weekly or twice per week; aggregate trends for org health.

* Single question rotation (mood/workload/support)
* Response: emoji scale or 1–5
* Optional “add context” (private)
* Aggregation thresholds (minimum N responses before showing team-level data)
* Trend charts and rolling averages

### 9.3 OOO / Vacation Calendar

**Purpose:** Make availability visible and reduce friction.

* Add OOO dates (or sync later)
* View team availability
* Optional: show on profile card

### 9.4 Professional Challenges / Growth Nudges

**Purpose:** Build career momentum via gentle prompts.

* Daily/weekly/monthly challenges
* Personalized suggestions (skills, courses, stretch prompts)
* Save/complete tracking
* Optional: tie to goals

### 9.5 Voice Notes → Transcription → Structured Data

**Purpose:** Remove friction and enable “talk to your work companion.”

* Record voice note anywhere (Snaps, Goals, Feedback, Reflection)
* Transcribe and summarize
* Map to fields (e.g., goal title, keep/consider feedback)
* Confirm before saving (user review step)

### 9.6 Quarterly Self-Reflection / Self-Evaluation

**Purpose:** Deep reflection cadence tied to goals and growth.

* Prompt-based reflection
* Pull in goal progress + (optional) pulse trends
* Export/share with manager

### 9.7 Supervisor Feedback (Manager workflows)

* Manager feedback prompts
* Light approvals for goals visibility
* Team-level insights dashboard

---

## 10) Key User Journeys

### MVP Journey A — New user onboarding

1. Create profile → fun facts + strengths
2. Browse directory → discover teammates
3. Add first goal → set quarterly timeframe
4. Give first Snap → reinforce positive loop
5. Request feedback from a peer

### MVP Journey B — Weekly use

* Quick goal progress update
* Give a Snap to someone who helped
* Provide requested peer feedback

## 12) Permissions & Privacy

* Roles: Admin, Manager, Member
* Profile visibility: default org-wide
* Goals visibility: private/manager/team options
* Feedback anonymity rules: policy-configurable
* Pulse data: aggregated only; thresholds enforced
* Audit logging for sensitive actions (feedback reports, admin views)

---

## 13) Notifications

* Push + in-app notifications for:

  * Snap received
  * Feedback request received
  * Feedback received
  * Goal check-in reminder
  * (Phase 2+) Pulse prompt

Controls:

* User-level notification preferences
* Quiet hours (optional)

---

## 14) Analytics & Success Metrics

### Adoption

* DAU/WAU
* Profile completion rate
* First-week activation: create goal, give snap, request/provide feedback

### Culture

* Snaps per user per week
* Distribution of recognition across team (avoid “same few people”)

### Growth & Accountability

* Goals created per user
* % goals with weekly updates
* Feedback request fulfillment rate

### Org Health (Phase 2+)

* Pulse response rate
* Mood trend over time (rolling average)

---

## 15) UX / Visual Design Notes

* Minimal, high-contrast typography
* Clear spacing and hierarchy
* Elegant micro-interactions (subtle haptics, animations)
* Consistent components
* “Calm” empty states with friendly prompts

---

## 16) Technical Considerations (High-Level)

* Auth: email + SSO later (optional)
* Backend: REST/GraphQL; real-time updates optional
* Storage: secure media handling for photos/voice
* Transcription (Phase 3): on-device where possible; otherwise secure service
* Moderation/reporting workflow for feedback

---

## 17) Risks & Mitigations

* **Adoption risk:** Keep MVP tight; focus on habit loop (Snaps + Goals)
* **Feedback safety:** Clear guidelines, reporting, admin auditability
* **Privacy concerns:** Transparent policy and aggregated-only insights
* **Tool redundancy (Slack):** Position social board as “culture feed,” not chat

---

## 18) Open Questions

* Should goals be strictly quarterly or flexible timeframes?
* Should Snaps be visible org-wide by default or private?
* Anonymous feedback policy: allowed for everyone, or only via requests?
* Will Pulse be weekly or 2x/week by default?
* What’s the preferred tech stack and data hosting constraints?
